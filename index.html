About Me

Teacher by calling.
Builder by curiosity.

I'm Mr Reyy — an English language teacher from Puchong, Selangor, Malaysia with a growing passion for bringing digital innovation into the classroom.

Beyond teaching, I build digital tools for educators. My first: the CEFR Lesson Planner — a web app that generates internationally-benchmarked lesson plans in seconds. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, deployed live on Railway with AI assistance.

That project proved something: teachers can be makers. The tools we need don't always exist yet — and we're often the best people to build them.
