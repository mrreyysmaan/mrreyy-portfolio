About Me

Teacher by calling.
Builder by curiosity.

I'm Mr Reyy — an English language teacher based in Putrajaya, Malaysia. I graduated with a B.Ed TESL from IPG Kampus Bahasa Antarabangsa, Kuala Lumpur, and began my teaching journey in 2022. Four years in, I'm still here — still learning, still growing, and still finding new ways to make every lesson count.

The story behind the CEFR Lesson Planner begins with a real problem. When UPSR was abolished and PBD (Pentaksiran Bilik Darjah) took its place, teachers were handed the freedom to choose their own materials and lesson designs. That freedom is powerful — but with it came an honest challenge: planning differentiated lessons for Advanced, Intermediate, and Remedial learners, all at once, every single day. I struggled with it. So instead of waiting for a solution to appear, I built one.

That first build proved something important to me: teachers can be makers. The tools we need don't always exist yet — and educators are often the best people to create them. Beyond the classroom, I'm a certified Google Educator (Level 1 & 2), a national-level cricket coach, and someone who believes that reflection is what separates a good teacher from a great one.

The Origin Story

Why I Built It

When PBD replaced UPSR, teachers gained the freedom to design their own lessons — but with it came the daily challenge of catering to three different learner levels at once. I struggled with that. So I built the CEFR Lesson Planner — because the tools we need don't always exist yet, and teachers are often the best people to build them.
